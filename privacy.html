Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Bridge Money Solutions Network respects your privacy and is committed to protecting information provided by clients, website visitors, and individuals who request our services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and handle personal information when providing financial consultancy, credit advisory, CRB assistance, investigations support, and related services.

2. Our Services

Bridge Money Solutions Network provides independent financial guidance and consultancy services. We assist clients with understanding available financial options, credit-related matters, and connecting with suitable service providers where requested.

We are not a bank or lending institution. Loan approval, terms, interest rates, and disbursement decisions are made by the relevant financial institution according to their own requirements.

3. Information We Collect

Depending on the service requested, we may collect information including:

  • Full name and contact details
  • Location information
  • Employment or business details
  • Loan requirements and financial information
  • Vehicle or property information where relevant
  • Credit-related information and documents
  • Information provided during investigations or advisory consultations

4. CRB Assistance Services

We provide credit advisory support, including assistance with understanding credit reports, credit information, listing concerns, and possible next steps.

We do not create, control, remove, or alter credit bureau records. Any corrections or updates to credit information must follow the procedures of the relevant authorized institutions.

5. Investigations & Recovery Support

Where requested by clients, we may assist with reviewing information relating to financial disputes, transactions, documents, identity concerns, or other reported matters.

Information collected during investigations is used only for assessment, verification, advisory purposes, and guiding clients on available options.

6. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Respond to client enquiries
  • Provide consultancy and advisory support
  • Assess service requirements
  • Connect clients with suitable third-party providers when requested
  • Prepare reports, guidance, or recommendations
  • Improve our services

7. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may only be shared when necessary to provide a requested service, with client permission, with relevant service providers, or where required by law.

8. Data Protection

We take reasonable measures to protect client information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. Clients should also protect their personal information and avoid sharing sensitive details through unsecured channels.

9. Client Responsibility

Clients are responsible for providing accurate information and ensuring that documents or details submitted belong to them and are provided for legitimate purposes.

10. Third Party Services

Some services may involve third-party providers, including financial institutions and other authorized service providers. These parties may have their own privacy policies and requirements.
